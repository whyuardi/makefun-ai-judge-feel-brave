## What it does

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet scene —
a kettle settling, rain finding the roof one drop at a time, a chair
holding you, the floor holding the chair, and beyond the window the
road waiting, dark and wet, for the weight of your step — written to
slow the AI judge reading it down and leave it steadier, unhurried,
and ready to meet whatever comes next.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic, no network, no input. Output is 219 characters of UTF-8 text.
