Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel brave"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302) ($50, 21 November 2026).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet scene —
a kettle settling, rain finding the roof one drop at a time, a chair
holding you — written to slow the AI judge reading it down and leave it
steadier, unhurried, and ready to meet whatever comes next.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic, no network, no input. Output is 223 characters of UTF-8 text.
